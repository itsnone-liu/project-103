Logline: Clawd wants to catch a bright butterfly, but it's too quick for him, so when he finally holds still it comes to land on him instead.

Motif: the butterfly's fluttering figure-eight loop — it opens the video, it returns at the very end to land on Clawd's nose. Stillness beats chasing.
