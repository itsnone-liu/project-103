# STORYBOARD — "The Butterfly" (15.0 s)

**Logline:** Clawd wants to catch the meadow's one butterfly, but it out-flies every pounce, so when he finally gives up and holds still, the butterfly lands on him instead.

**World:** A dewy morning meadow. Cool sage-teal grasses, pale blue-green sky, scattered ochre and cream flowers; the butterfly is rose-violet with cream spots — the brightest thing in every shot. Low sun glows from the right (`glow`), and the light climbs and warms across the video: cool mist at 0 s → full golden cream by 15 s, tracking Clawd's mood.

**Motif:** The butterfly's **loop** — it arrives flying loops (A), dodges the pounce with a loop (C), and says goodbye with one big farewell loop (E). Secondary: the **perch** — it sits on a flower (B), then chooses Clawd's head (D); the perch does the choosing.

**Clawd's arc (emotion keys):** neutral → surprised → hopeful (A) · mischief → determined (B) · surprised → dizzy → confused → hopeful (C) · surprised → shy → love (D) · relieved → laugh → happy (E). Cold-to-warm body tint follows the light.
